🛡️ Safety Measures

Simple ways to protect yourself from common cyber threats.

🎣

Phishing

  • Verify the sender before responding.
  • Don't click suspicious links or attachments.
  • Never share OTPs, passwords, or PINs.
  • Check the website URL carefully.
  • Report suspicious messages.
More Information →
🦠

Malware

  • Keep your operating system and apps updated.
  • Use trusted antivirus/security software.
  • Download software only from trusted sources.
  • Don't open unknown attachments.
  • Scan external USB drives before using them.
More Information →
🔒

Ransomware

  • Keep regular backups of important files.
  • Keep software and security tools updated.
  • Avoid suspicious links and attachments.
  • Use strong passwords and MFA.
  • Don't install software from unknown sources.
More Information →
👤

Social Engineering

  • Be careful with unexpected requests.
  • Verify someone's identity before sharing information.
  • Never reveal confidential information under pressure.
  • Keep personal information private.
  • Take time to think before acting on urgent requests.
More Information →
🔑

Password Attacks

  • Use long, unique passwords.
  • Never reuse the same password everywhere.
  • Use a password manager.
  • Enable multi-factor authentication (MFA).
  • Never share your passwords with others
More Information →
💳

Online Scams

  • Verify offers before making payments.
  • Don't trust "too good to be true" deals.
  • Avoid sending money to unknown people.
  • Never share OTPs, PINs, or banking details.
  • Use trusted websites and payment platforms.
More Information →
🌐

Unsafe Browsing

  • Check for HTTPS before entering sensitive information.
  • Avoid suspicious websites and pop-ups.
  • Don't download files from unknown websites.
  • Keep your browser updated.
  • Use browser security and privacy settings.
More Information →
📱

Mobile Security

  • Lock your phone with a strong PIN/password.
  • Install apps only from official app stores.
  • Review app permissions regularly.
  • Keep your phone and apps updated.
  • Avoid connecting to unknown devices or networks.
More Information →
📵

SIM-Swap Fraud

  • Set a SIM PIN if supported by your provider.
  • Contact your mobile provider if your SIM suddenly stops working.
  • Never share OTPs, PINs, or verification codes.
  • Use an authenticator app instead of SMS for important accounts when possible.
  • Ask your bank to alert you about unusual account activity.
More Information →
📡

Public Wi-Fi Risks

  • Verify the network name before connecting.
  • Avoid banking on unsecured public Wi-Fi.
  • Use a VPN on untrusted networks.
  • Turn off automatic Wi-Fi connection.
  • Don't enter sensitive information on suspicious websites.
More Information →
🎭

Deepfake & AI Scams

  • Don't automatically trust AI-generated voices or videos.
  • Verify unusual requests through another communication method.
  • Don't transfer money based only on a voice/video/message.
  • Cross-check important information with trusted sources.
  • Don't share sensitive information with unknown AI tools.
More Information →
🕵️

Identity Theft

  • Use strong, unique passwords and MFA.
  • Don't publicly share sensitive personal information.
  • Monitor bank and online accounts for unusual activity.
  • Store identity documents securely.
  • Report suspected identity theft quickly.
